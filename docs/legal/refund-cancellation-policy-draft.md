# HerBook Consultation Cancellation & Refund Policy
**Document Version**: 1.0-DRAFT  
**Statutory Framework**: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 & Indian Contract Act, 1872  
**Last Updated**: October 16, 2026  
**Status**: DRAFT — INTERNAL REVIEW

---

## 1. Scope
This Policy governs cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds for appointments booked through HerCare teleconsultation services.

---

## 2. Patient Cancellation Window & Refund Eligibility
1. **Cancellation More Than 4 Hours Prior to Slot**:
   - If a patient cancels an upcoming appointment more than 4 hours before the scheduled appointment start time, a **100% full refund** of the consultation fee (₹500) and platform fee (₹50) shall be credited back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.
2. **Cancellation Within 4 Hours of Slot**:
   - Cancellations initiated less than 4 hours prior to the scheduled start time are subject to a cancellation fee of 50% of the practitioner fee to compensate the practitioner for reserved clinic time, unless substantiated by a sudden medical emergency.
3. **Patient No-Show**:
   - If a patient fails to join the virtual consultation room within 10 minutes of the scheduled time without prior notification, the booking shall be marked as "No-Show" and no refund will be issued.

---

## 3. Practitioner Cancellation or Technical Disruption
1. **Practitioner Unavailability**:
   - If a practitioner is unable to attend due to an emergency or clinic exigency, the patient will be notified immediately and offered an instant free reschedule or a 100% full refund.
2. **Technical Connectivity Failure**:
   - If the video consultation fails due to technical failure of HerBook's server infrastructure or the doctor's connection, a replacement consultation will be arranged at no additional charge, or a full refund issued upon review.

---

## 4. Refund Processing Timeframes
All approved refunds are initiated through our RBI-licensed payment gateway partners (e.g., Razorpay / Cashfree) and reflected in the patient's bank account or UPI handle within 5 to 7 banking working days.
