## Informed Patient Consent Acknowledgment

By booking and entering a virtual consultation on HerBook, you (the patient or authorized caregiver) confirm and agree to the following terms:

### 1. Purpose & Modality
I understand that this teleconsultation is conducted over end-to-end encrypted real-time video/audio technology for non-emergency clinical evaluation, follow-up, hormonal advice, or second opinions with an independent Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP).

### 2. Clinical Inherent Limitations
I acknowledge that:
- A teleconsultation is fundamentally different from an in-person physical clinical visit.
- The practitioner cannot perform physical palpation, pelvic examinations, or direct diagnostic physical maneuvers over video.
- Diagnostic accuracy relies upon the quality of video/audio connectivity and the accuracy of information, symptoms, and medical reports provided by me.

### 3. Practitioner Authority to Direct In-Person Care
I understand that the practitioner holds full clinical discretion to decide whether my condition can be safely evaluated via telemedicine. If the practitioner deems in-person examination, hospital admission, or diagnostic ultrasound necessary, they may terminate the video consultation and direct me to an appropriate healthcare facility.

### 4. Emergency Exclusion
**I certify that I am NOT experiencing a medical emergency.**
I acknowledge that teleconsultation is inappropriate for:
- Severe acute pelvic pain or sudden unbearable abdominal agony
- Heavy uncontrolled vaginal hemorrhaging (soaking more than two pads per hour)
- High fever accompanied by pelvic tenderness or altered consciousness
- Suspected ectopic pregnancy rupture or acute surgical abdomen

In any such emergency, I agree to seek immediate treatment at the nearest hospital emergency department or call national emergency numbers (`112` or `108`).

### 5. Privacy, Confidentiality & Prohibited Recordings
- I understand that my consultation session is private and confidential.
- Both the patient and the practitioner are strictly prohibited from taking unauthorized screen captures, video recordings, or audio recordings of the consultation without explicit mutual written consent.
- Any shared medical records (ultrasound reports, blood work) are accessible to the practitioner solely for the duration of the consultation and clinical follow-up.
