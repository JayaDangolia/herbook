## 2. Mandatory Practitioner Warranties & Statutory Registration
The Practitioner expressly warrants and covenants that:
1. They hold a recognized medical degree (MBBS, MS, MD, BAMS, BHMS) from a university recognized under Indian law.
2. They are enrolled on the State Medical Register, National Medical Commission Register, NCISM Register, or NCH Register with active, valid, and unencumbered licensure.
3. They shall immediately inform the Platform in writing if their license is suspended, revoked, or subject to formal council disciplinary proceedings.
4. They shall adhere strictly to their statutory scope of practice (e.g., Ayurvedic practitioners shall practice in accordance with NCISM norms; Allopathic practitioners shall adhere to NMC regulations; Homeopathic practitioners shall not prescribe Allopathic pharmaceuticals).

---

## 3. Telemedicine Compliance (Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020)
1. **Patient Identification & Consent**: The Practitioner shall verify patient identity and confirm informed consent at the commencement of each consultation.
2. **Clinical Judgment & In-Person Care Right**: The Practitioner retains sole clinical autonomy to determine whether teleconsultation is appropriate. If physical examination, ultrasound, or emergency care is required, the Practitioner **must immediately advise in-person clinical consultation**.
3. **Prescription Limitations**:
   - The Practitioner shall prescribe only within approved List O, List A, or List B categories under the Telemedicine Guidelines.
   - **Prohibited Drugs**: The Practitioner shall **NEVER prescribe Schedule X medications**, psychiatric injectables, habit-forming narcotics, or emergency abortifacients via teleconsultation.
   - Every digital prescription must cite the Practitioner's full name, registered qualifications, council registration number, clinic address, and digital signature.
4. **Prohibition of AI Autonomous Prescriptions**: The Practitioner warrants that all clinical advice represents their own direct human clinical judgment. Autonomous AI diagnosing or automated prescription bots are strictly prohibited.

## 4. Financial Terms, Consultation Fees & Platform Margins
1. The agreed consultation fee for pilot consultations is ₹500 (Rupees Five Hundred Only) payable to the Practitioner, and a platform booking fee of ₹50 (Rupees Fifty Only) retained by HerBook.
2. Settlement shall occur within 7 business days following consultation completion.
3. **No Fee Sharing for Referrals**: In accordance with the Indian Medical Council (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations, 2002, the platform booking fee represents technological software maintenance and payment processing only, and does NOT constitute an illegal fee-splitting or kickback commission for medical referrals.

## 5. Indemnification & Medical Malpractice
The Practitioner maintains full professional liability for all diagnoses, clinical advice, and treatment plans rendered to patients. The Practitioner agrees to maintain valid medical professional indemnity insurance coverage throughout their tenure on the Platform and shall indemnify HerBook against any medical negligence or malpractice claims arising from their clinical consultations.
