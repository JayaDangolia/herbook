# HerBook Health Creator Agreement & Verification Standards

---

## 1. Scope
This Agreement governs all creators, health educators, and medical influencers who publish short-form video reels, articles, or educational graphics on HerBook (HerReels and HerWell).

---

## 2. Creator Verification & Medical Badge Eligibility
1. **Medical Professional Badge ("Verified Clinician")**:
   - Granted exclusively to creators who hold verified statutory medical registration (MBBS, MD, MS, BAMS, BHMS) verified against the NMC/NCISM/NCH registers.
   - Non-medically licensed creators shall NEVER be granted clinical verification badges.
2. **General Health Educators**:
   - Certified nutritionists, yoga instructors, and fitness coaches must submit verifiable certification and are designated as "Wellness Creator", clearly distinguishing them from licensed medical doctors.

---

## 3. Statutory Advertising & Sponsored Content Disclosures (ASCI 2021)
1. **Mandatory Tagging**: If a reel or article promotes a menstrual cup brand, heating pad, nutritional supplement, or commercial service in exchange for monetary compensation, free products, or affiliate commission, the creator **must prominently display `#Ad` or `#Sponsored`** in the video overlay and caption.
2. **No Promotion of Unapproved Drugs**: Creators shall NOT promote Schedule H, H1, or X pharmaceuticals, weight-loss injections, or guaranteed fertility concoctions.

---

## 4. Content Moderation & Striking Policy
HerBook maintains a three-strike policy for health misinformation:
- **Strike 1**: Removal of flagged video and formal warning.
- **Strike 2**: 14-day publishing freeze.
- **Strike 3**: Permanent revocation of creator publishing permissions and badge removal.
