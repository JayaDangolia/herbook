## 1. The HerCircle Sisterhood Mission
HerCircle is a safe, empathetic, and confidential sanctuary for women to discuss reproductive health, menstrual wellbeing, PCOS, fertility journeys, maternal care, and mental health without stigma, shame, or judgment.

To preserve this safe haven, all participants must strictly abide by these Guidelines.

---

## 2. Zero-Tolerance Violations (Immediate Removal & Account Ban)

1. **Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII) & Sexual Abuse**:
   - Posting explicit nudity, intimate photos, voyeuristic media, or soliciting sexual favors is strictly prohibited and results in immediate permanent ban and referral to Indian cyber-crime authorities under Section 66E and 67 of the IT Act.
2. **Harassment, Cyber-Bullying & Doxxing**:
   - Targeted insults, body-shaming, threats of harm, sharing another person's private phone number, address, or medical history without authorization.
3. **Medical Misinformation & Unproven "Miracle Cures"**:
   - Promising guaranteed cures for infertility, cancer, or PCOS using unproven tonics or extreme fasting.
   - Advertising prescription pharmaceuticals, hormonal injections, or off-label abortion pills without a licensed clinical prescription.
   - Anti-vaccine conspiracy claims or discouraging emergency medical care.
4. **Hate Speech & Discriminatory Content**:
   - Attacks or slurs based on religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or regional origin.

## 3. Four-Tier Medical Content Taxonomy

To maintain clinical integrity, content on HerBook is categorized into four distinct tiers:
1. **Personal Experience**: User-generated narratives sharing subjective lived experiences (e.g., "What helped my PCOS cramps"). Must clearly be labeled as peer experience, NOT medical advice.
2. **General Educational**: Informational wellness content grounded in public health resources.
3. **Professionally Reviewed**: Evidence-based clinical articles and videos authored or audited by verified Registered Medical Practitioners displaying their council credentials.
4. **Sponsored / Commercial**: Paid promotional content or affiliate links, requiring explicit statutory `#Ad` / `#Sponsored` disclosure tags under Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) guidelines.

---

## 4. Anonymity & Incognito Posting Safeguards
HerBook allows members to post anonymously ("Post Incognito") on sensitive topics such as intimate wellness or pregnancy loss. 
- When Incognito mode is selected, the platform strips the author's real name, email, and user avatar from public API feeds.
- Anonymity does NOT protect abusive or illegal posts; backend audit logs retain tamper-evident identifiers to comply with court orders or cyber-police warrants under Section 69 of the IT Act.

---

## 5. Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Reporting
If you witness a violation:
- Tap the **Flag / Report** icon on any post or comment.
- Select from our 6 specific reporting categories:
  1. Harassment or Cyber-stalking
  2. Impersonation
  3. Non-Consensual Intimate Media
  4. Dangerous Medical Misinformation / Unverified Cure
  5. Hate Speech or Slurs
  6. Commercial Spam / Selling Products
- All flagged items are forwarded to the HerBook 24/7 Moderation Committee for review within 24 hours pursuant to the IT Intermediary Rules, 2021.
